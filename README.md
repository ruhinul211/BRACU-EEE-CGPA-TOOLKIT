# EEE CGPA Toolkit

A free, all-in-one CGPA toolkit for BRAC University EEE (Version 3) students — runs entirely in your browser, no backend, no accounts, nothing ever uploaded anywhere.

**Live site:** https://ruhinul211.github.io/BRACU-EEE-CGPA-TOOLKIT/

## What it does

- **Gradesheet Analyzer** — upload your BRACU unofficial gradesheet PDF and it auto-fills your whole plan, parsed entirely client-side.
- **Calculate Raw CGPA** — quick calculator, no course names needed.
- **Semester Wise CGPA** — the full Version 3 sequence, semester by semester, with automatic retake/repeat detection and correct BRACU CGPA math.
- **Horizon** — set a target CGPA and see what average you need, or try your own grade mix.
- **Should I Repeat?** — see the maximum CGPA you could reach with vs. without repeating any course below 4.00.
- Print/export a plan styled after BRACU's own unofficial gradesheet layout.
- Everything auto-saves to your browser's local storage; export/import to move it between devices.

## Disclaimer

This is an unofficial, student-built tool — not affiliated with, produced by, or endorsed by BRAC University. Always confirm your CGPA, credits, and any retake/repeat decision against your official gradesheet and your academic advisor.
