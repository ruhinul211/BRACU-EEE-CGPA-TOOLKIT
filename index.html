How BRACU calculates your CGPA

BRAC University uses a credit-weighted average. Each course contributes its grade point multiplied by its credit hours, and the total is divided by the credits attempted — so a 3-credit course moves your CGPA three times as much as a 1-credit lab.

GPA = ∑ (Grade point × Credits) ∑ Credits attempted

Your CGPA is the same formula applied across every course you've taken so far, not an average of your semester GPAs. That's why this calculator asks for your completed credits as well as your current CGPA — it reconstructs your total quality points as CGPA × completed credits, adds this semester's, and divides by the new credit total.

Grades carrying no grade point — P (Pass), I (Incomplete) and W (Withdrawal) — are left out of the calculation entirely.

  • An F in a course may be retaken twice. A third attempt is possible only by exception, arranged by your Head of Department or Dean with the Registrar (for example, to protect a student's progression).
  • Non-credit courses (091/092) taken in the first or second semester may be retaken only once.
  • Beyond that, further attempts need the Head of Department's permission in consultation with the Registrar, and only where major circumstances beyond your control applied.
  • The best grade you earn is the one counted toward your CGPA.
  • A course may be repeated once to improve the grade.
  • It must be repeated within 2 semesters of when you first enrolled in it. The Residential Semester (RS) doesn't count toward those two — so a course taken in Semester 01 can still be repeated in Semester 04, since Semester 03 (RS) is skipped.
  • There is no cap on the grade you can earn in the repeated course.
  • The latest grade counts toward your CGPA — not the best one.
The key difference: a retake (after an F) keeps your best grade, but a repeat (to improve a pass) keeps your most recent grade. If a repeat goes worse than your original, the lower grade is what stays on your CGPA. The Repeat Analytics tab models exactly this risk.

You may still see an older rule online capping retakes and repeats at B+ (from the Summer 2019 clarification). BRACU's current published policy states there is no cap and that it applies to all students — but if you're planning around this, confirm with the Registrar first.

The full Version 3 sequence, semester by semester — a manual, detailed alternative to the quick calculator, for anyone who wants to plan out every course by name. Jump straight to a semester, or search for a course.
This tab defaults to the official Version 3 recommended sequence and its 12 semesters, but nothing here is locked: add, remove, or swap any course in any semester, and add semesters beyond 12 if your own journey runs longer. Should I Repeat?

Every course below 4.00 shows up here. Pick a hoped-for grade to see the maximum CGPA you could reach with vs. without repeating it, and exactly how many points the repeat is worth. This tab is pure analysis — once you've decided, go implement the repeat yourself in Semester Wise CGPA by picking the same course again in a later semester.
BRACU rule: you may repeat a course once to improve its grade, within 2 semesters of first taking it — the Residential Semester doesn't count toward those two. There's no cap on the new grade, and whichever grade you get most recently is the one that counts, even if it's lower.
